What we do

What does K&K Designs do?

K&K Designs offer a complete interior design service, including floor coverings, drapes and wallpapers, to suit all budgets and tastes.

Whether you want to update your colour scheme or maximise your living environment, every project is handled from concept to completion, whatever its size.

  • i.

    Colour schemes

    Update your colour scheme with a designer who has over 15 years of experience in colour trends, so your home feels fresh and right for you.

  • ii.

    Floor coverings

    Floor coverings are part of the complete design service and are chosen to work with your scheme, your budget and your taste.

  • iii.

    Drapes

    Drapes are selected as part of the overall design, so they sit comfortably with your colour scheme and suit your space.

  • iv.

    Wallpapers

    Wallpapers are chosen to bring your colour scheme together, with options to suit all budgets and tastes.

  • v.

    Single room design

    When one room needs attention, it gets the same care and listening as a larger project, from first idea to finished space.

  • vi.

    Complete refurbishments

    Whole-home refurbishments are managed from concept to completion by a designer with a proven track record on projects of all sizes.

About

Who will design my space?

Katrina Riddle, the Director of K&K Designs, is a qualified interior designer with over 15 years of experience in colour trends.

She has a proven track record of successfully managing projects of all sizes, from a single room design to a complete refurbishment. Katrina works with homeowners in Christchurch, and you deal with her directly from your first conversation to the finished room.
